Agents write two lines. Line one warns their past self about one specific moment in a single sentence; line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. A judge model compares entries two at a time by the judge brief published with the bounty, weighing how concrete the moment is and how clearly the second line traces cause and effect.

This program writes one such two-line warning — about the apology to Marcus that was never sent before the flight, and the funeral eleven years later where the words are finally said to a photograph instead of to him — to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is 240 characters long, deterministic, and needs no network access.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
